# Read This First — The Series Corrections Sheet

**Series:** *Raising Our Children as God Intended | A Guide To Islamic Child Raising (Tarbiyah)*
**Speaker:** Shaykh Usama Abdulghani · Channel: Light Of Guidance · 16 parts, ~14 hours
**Playlist:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6wktQtsoJqwoAJaZWRfQHZQfdzw8Mn5D

This one page is the distillation of sixteen full analysis documents. It exists so that you can benefit from the series **without repeating its errors** — because what you repeat to your children, they will repeat to theirs, and one day one of them will look it up.

> Every item below was checked against the Arabic primary sources: al-Kafi on thaqalayn.com (with al-Majlisi's gradings in *Mir'at al-'Uqul* and Behbudi's in *Sahih al-Kafi*), *Tafsir al-Mizan* in Arabic, *Majma' al-Bayan*, Nahj al-Balagha, *Wasa'il al-Shi'a*, *Tuhaf al-'Uqul*, the *maqatil* literature, dorar.net for the Sunni corpus, and the peer-reviewed literature for the empirical claims.

## The one finding that explains all the others

**His book-learned material is genuinely good. His orally-transmitted material is where the failures cluster.**

When Shaykh Usama cites al-Kafi with a chapter and a hadith number, he is usually right, often more precise than the popular English translations, and occasionally *under-claiming* the strength of his own source. When he tells a story he heard, quotes a contemporary figure from memory, or reaches for a maxim that "is hadith now," the failure rate rises sharply.

This is a fact about **method**, not about honesty. He repeatedly flags his own fiction before telling it, defers objections he cannot answer, and once — at the most tempting moment in the whole series — declines to invent a hadith and argues from a named principle instead. A parent who knows the pattern can use the series with real confidence: **trust the citations, verify the stories.**

A second, structural finding explains the maxims. Nine of the *karam* sayings across Parts 14–16 map entry-for-entry, in order, onto a single topical anthology built on *Ghurar al-Hikam*. *Ghurar* carries no chains. That is why his maxims are accurately quoted but never chain-graded — the source he works from has nothing to grade.

## ⚠ Right text, wrong speaker or wrong frame

- **«من قدّم أولاداً» is bereavement literature, not a parenting promise.** Used in **Parts 1, 2 and 7** as a promise that well-raised children shield you from the Fire. The text is real (*Thawab al-A'mal* p.178 → *Bihar* 82) but sits in a chapter titled ***Ajr al-Masa'ib*** — "The Reward of Calamities" — and *qaddama* means children who **predeceased** the parent. Chain weak.
- **The Jewish onlooker who objects to al-Husayn (a) on the Prophet's back does not exist.** Used in **Parts 2 and 8**. The authentic objector, rebuked with *man la yarham la yurham*, is **al-Aqra' b. Habis — a Muslim Arab chieftain** (Bukhari 5997). The authentic version aims the rebuke *inside* the community, which is the more useful version. To the Shaykh's credit, in Part 3 he flags this himself.
- **The "free play → *halim*" payoff is not in the 7-7-7 chapter.** The *hilm* text exists, but as a **separate, weaker narration from a different Imam** — al-Kafi 6:51 ch.37 h.2, **Imam al-Kazim (a)**, al-Majlisi: ***majhul***. And its key word is *'aramah* (boisterousness), not "energy." The circulating corollary — *"be worried about a quiet, compliant child"* — **is not in the Arabic at all.** Do not tell the parent of a quiet child that an Imam diagnosed a problem.
- **The 7-7-7 hadith promises nothing.** The stage division is sound (al-Kafi 6:47 h.3, ***muwaththaq*** / Behbudi ***sahih***). But both outcome-bearing versions carry an **explicit failure branch** — «فإن أفلح وإلا فإنه ممن لا خير فيه» — which is omitted every time it is quoted. The tradition never guaranteed the result.
- **"The child *becomes* mannerly, you don't make him mannerly"** (Part 4) is false of every extant version. No text uses Form V *yata'addab*; the three-clause form uses **يُؤَدَّب**, a passive requiring an agent, and al-Kafi 6:47 h.2 is blunter still: **فَأَدِّبْهُ بِأَدَبِكَ** — an imperative to the father.
- **«من لم يجاز الإساءة بالإحسان فليس من الكرام»** is ***Ghurar al-Hikam* 8958, Imam 'Ali (a)** — attributed to the Prophet (s) in **Part 15** and correctly to 'Ali (a) in **Part 16**.
- **The two-loaves story is Imam al-Sajjad's (a) throughout**, not Imam al-Sadiq's (a) — *Muntaha al-Amal* 2:38–39 (Part 16).
- **Both Part 14 lying-hadith are Imam al-Baqir's (a)**, not "our Prophet" — al-Kafi 2 ch.139 h.3 (*muwaththaq*) and h.4 (*da'if*).
- **Nahj al-Balagha Letter 31 was not written across town** (Part 3). al-Sharif al-Radi's own heading: written **returning from Siffin, at Hadirin**. The advice to write to your child survives; the premise that made it evidence does not.
- **«لا رأي لمن لا يطاع» is Nahj Khutba 27** — Imam 'Ali's (a) **lament** after the raid on al-Anbar, about his own unobeyed authority. Not a prescription for obedience-training (Part 8).
- **Q 35:14 is not a maxim about deferring to human expertise** (Part 12). The *khabir* is God; the subject is the futility of shirk.
- **Three "the *fuqaha* say" claims are not fiqh:** that a child's damage need not be replaced (Part 5); that the man is the *wali* of the house (Part 11 — *wilayah* over persons attaches to the father and paternal grandfather over minors; the real category is *qiwamah*); and that housework is the wife's duty (Part 4 — **Sistani, *Islamic Laws* ruling 2432**: a man has no right to compel his wife to do housework). In each case the practical advice he draws is *stronger* without the false warrant.

## ⚠ The numbers to correct

| Claim | Where | The literature |
|---|---|---|
| Gaming addiction is 10–30% of children | Part 13 | **3.05% / 1.96%** (Stevens et al., *ANZJP* 2021); **0.3–1.0%** (Przybylski et al., *AJP* 2017). Off by an order of magnitude. |
| Sugar makes children hyperactive | Part 7 | 23 double-blind RCTs clear sugar (*JAMA* 1995). The real culprits are **artificial colourings + sodium benzoate** (*Lancet* 2007) and caffeine — both in his own Cheetos-and-soda example. The strongest finding is that the expectancy effect sits in the **parent** (Hoover & Milich 1994). |
| "Michigan gives you twelve weeks of maternity leave" | Part 5 | Michigan has no such statute; it is **federal FMLA, unpaid**, with eligibility thresholds many workers fail. **Iran — where he sets the story — mandates nine months paid.** The real figure served his argument better. |
| Screens will make a child stop wanting to play | Part 9 | Displacement is real but **small** (r ≈ −0.1). "Grit" and "instant gratification" are unsupported here and both constructs have taken replication damage (Credé 2017; Watts 2018). Mood effects are real, small and **bidirectional** (Orben & Przybylski 2019: ≈0.4% of variance). |
| Lazy eye: the window slams shut | Part 9 | You patch the **strong** eye, and **PEDIG (*Arch Ophthalmol* 2005) found children aged 7–17 still respond.** The window narrows; it does not close. |
| Restricting play breaks sibling relationships | Part 10 | No evidence links play restriction to sibling relationship breakdown. This is the load-bearing beam of his longest case study. |
| Siblings should be no more than three years apart | Part 4 | Not evidence-based (*Frontiers in Pediatrics* 2022 review: no optimal interval). He correctly hedges it to "some scholars." |

**Two he gets right, and they are his best empirical moments.** *Parental controls are a failed experiment* — **Przybylski & Nash 2018** (counterweight: Collier 2016 puts restrictive mediation at a small-but-real r+ = −.06). And the punitive-school lying study — **Talwar & Lee, *Child Development* 2011;82(6):1751–58** — is his Part 14 thesis, empirically confirmed: children in the punitive school lied *more*, and lied *better*.

## ⚠ On the attributions to Ayatollah Khamenei — and an honesty note

Six attributions were checked. **Two verified:** the "nothing can take the place of a book" remark (35th Tehran Book Fair, **14 May 2024**), and the *taqriz* on «تنها گریه کن», the memoir of Shahid Memarian's mother, unveiled **21 November 2021**.

The rest could not be confirmed — **but english.khamenei.ir, farsi.khamenei.ir and leader.ir all became 403-blocked partway through this work**, along with several Persian news archives. A negative from that point on means *the archive could not be reached*, not *he did not say it*. That distinction is kept in every document and should be kept when you talk about it.

| A weak "let the child play" citation | **al-Kafi 6:47 h.3** — ***muwaththaq*** / Behbudi ***sahih*** — the soundest text in the field, and the one where the child genuinely is the subject of every verb. |

**And the single strongest citation in the entire series, which he mentions and then throws away** (Part 3):

> **«إذا أحببت رجلاً فأخبره بذلك، فإنه أثبت للمودة بينكما»**
> *"If you love someone, tell him so — it is more binding on the affection between you."*
> — al-Kafi, *bab i'lam al-akh bi'l-hubb*, h.2. Graded ***sahih* by al-Majlisi AND by Behbudi**, on a doubled chain of *thiqat*. There is nothing sounder in the field.

## How to use the sixteen documents

Each `lecture-NN-*.md` file carries the full seventeen-section treatment: executive summary, series connection, detailed breakdown with timestamps, top-ten takeaways, verse-by-verse al-Mizan analysis, narration-by-narration verification with named graders, an audit table, a parenting action guide, family reflection questions, activities written specifically for **Abbas (7)** and **Eliyya (4)**, a character map, a framework, common mistakes, a memorisation sheet, a journal, golden nuggets, and a confidence-and-accuracy close.

**Section 7 of each document is the one to read before you repeat anything from that lecture.** Section 14 is safe to memorise — nothing unverified was allowed onto it.

The supporting research lives in `research/`: `_verified-index.md` is the shared verification index with all three addenda, and `lecture-NN-audit.md` is the raw source audit behind each document. `transcripts/` holds the auto-caption transcripts every timestamp refers to.

**A closing word on fairness.** It would be easy to read this page and conclude the series is unreliable. That would be the wrong conclusion. Fourteen hours of material produced roughly forty items needing correction — and produced, alongside them, dozens of correctly cited narrations, several al-Mizan readings that are exactly right, a genuinely excellent grasp of the *tarbiyah* literature's structure, and a pastoral instinct that is often better than his sourcing. Part 16, the last, is his best-sourced hour of the whole series. The corrections above are the price of using it well, not a verdict against it.
