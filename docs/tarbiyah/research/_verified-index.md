# Shared verification index — Tarbiyah series

Findings already established by deep verification during the Part 1 and Part 2 audits.
**Read this before verifying anything.** Several of these narrations recur across many
lectures; if an item you are checking appears here, cite this entry rather than
re-researching it, and spend your effort on citations NOT listed here.

## Confidence vocabulary used throughout
- **Strongly verified** — text, source and reference confirmed in a primary collection.
- **Moderately verified** — located, but chainless (*mursal*), weak in chain, or the exact reference could not be pinned.
- **Needs further verification** — not located, or the attribution is unsettled.

# ADDENDUM 3 — established during the Parts 13-16 audits

This addendum was written after the Parts 13, 14, 15 and 16 audits completed. Parts 14 and 15 were finished in a second pass after a session rate limit truncated the first. Read this section before writing any of Parts 13-16 — several items below **correct** entries above.

## ⭐ Documented forgeries and non-hadith (do NOT repeat as narrated)

- **The Tabuk hand-kiss** ("the Prophet kissed the calloused hand of the labourer and said this hand the Fire will never touch") — graded ***mawdu'*** (fabricated) by **Ibn al-Jawzi** and ***kadhib*** by **al-Dhahabi**. Part 13. Do not use. The underlying *value* (dignity of manual labour) is well supported elsewhere; the story is not.
- **"Intellect is the imam of the body, imagination is its soldier"** — **NOT a hadith.** The lineage is philosophical: **Ibn Sina → al-Ghazali → Imam Khomeini's *Sharh*.** Part 13. Cite it as a philosophical maxim if at all, never as a narration.
- ⭐⭐ **«لا يكذب الكاذب إلا من مهانة نفسه»** ("None lies but out of the lowliness of his own soul") — the load-bearing sentence of Part 14 — **is not a hadith.** Absent from al-Kafi's 22-hadith *bab al-kidhb*; dorar.net returns «لا توجد نتائج» for «مهانة نفسه». Its only traceable home is a **chapter heading in Ibn 'Adi's *al-Kamil fi Ma'rifat Du'afa' al-Muhaddithin*, bab 23** — «الكاذب يكذب صراحاً من مهانة نفسه عليه، والظريف لا يكذب» — a 4th/10th-c. hadith critic's dictum about why narrators fabricate, not a saying of the Prophet or an Imam.
  - ⭐ **Substitute:** **al-Kafi 2, *bab al-kibr*, h.17** — «ما من رجل تكبر أو تجبر إلا لذلة وجدها في نفسه» (Imam al-Sadiq). Identical psychological move — outward vice as compensation for inward lowliness — real Imam, real collection, gradeable chain.
- ⭐ **«أدِّ الأمانةَ إلى من ائتمنك ولا تخن من خانك»** — the opening Prophetic hadith of Part 15, introduced as "This is from Rasulullah" and used as the anchor of the whole *khiyanah* section. The dorar.net takhrij is unanimous and severe: **Ahmad: *batil*** · **Ibn al-Jawzi: all three Companion routes invalid** · **Abu Hatim: *munkar*** · **al-Shafi'i via al-Bayhaqi: *laysa bi-thabit*** · **Ibn Hazm: *la yasihh***. Only al-Tirmidhi 1264 dissents (*hasan gharib*). Sunni in provenance; **no Imami chain located.** ⭐ **Substitute:** Q 4:58 (*inna Allaha ya'murukum an tu'addu al-amanati ila ahliha*) + Q 5:8.
- **«الكذب والخيانة ليسا من أخلاق الكرام»** — **no attestation at all.** Substitute **Nahj al-Balagha Hikam 455**.
- **"If *husn al-khuluq* were a creation you could see…"** (Part 15, the hinge into the Behrouz Azimi near-death sequence) — **NOT LOCATED** in dorar or two Shia topical compilations. The nearest real text, «لو كان حسن الخلق رجلاً يمشي في الناس», is graded ***da'if*** and claims considerably less.
- **Muqaddas Ardabili and the *najasa* anecdote** (Part 14) — the man is correctly named (d. 993/1585, Najaf), though "Grand Ayatollah" is anachronistic for his period. The anecdote itself is **NOT LOCATED** — and this is a *completed* negative, since his anecdote corpus is well indexed.

## ⭐ Correct text, wrong attribution or wrong speaker

- **Both Part 14 lying-hadith are from Imam al-Baqir, not "our Prophet"** — al-Kafi 2, ch.139, h.3 (***muwaththaq***) and h.4 (***da'if***); al-Majlisi, *Mir'at al-'Uqul* 10/329.
- ⭐ **«من لم يجاز الإساءة بالإحسان فليس من الكرام»** — this is ***Ghurar al-Hikam* 8958, Imam 'Ali**. Part 15 attributes it to **the Prophet**; Part 16 attributes it correctly to 'Ali. Same speaker, two lectures, two attributions. Correct to 'Ali.
- ⭐ **The two-loaves story (Part 16) is Imam al-Sajjad's throughout, not Imam al-Sadiq's** — *Muntaha al-Amal* 2:38-39.
- ⭐ **Nahj al-Balagha Hikmah 89 is quoted at half length** (Part 14). «من كان له من نفسه واعظ كان عليه من الله حافظ» is the **second** clause. The **first** is «من أصلح ما بينه وبين الله أصلح الله ما بينه وبين الناس» — which supplies precisely the mechanism the lecture argues for but never names. Chainless (Nahj). Also circulates in Sunni homiletics as **al-Hasan al-Basri**; an al-Sadiq variant sits at *Mizan al-Hikmah* 4:788.
- ⭐ ***Makarim al-akhlaq* — Part 15 takes the weaker recension and loses the item his own lecture needed.** He gives the **three-item, addressed-to-'Ali** version, which is **Sunni and *da'if***. **al-Kafi 2/53/1** (al-Majlisi: ***hasan ka'l-sahih***) is a **public sermon with four items** — the fourth being *truthfulness even against yourself*, which is exactly the thread tying the closing section back to the opening case study of the boy who lies and steals. Use al-Kafi.
- **«إفشاؤك سر أخيك خيانة»** is **not a free-standing maxim** — it is a clause of the **Prophet's *wasiyyah* to Abu Dharr**. His attribution to the Prophet is therefore **correct**.
- **«لا تنظر إلى صغر الخطيئة…» — amendment to the earlier Bilal b. Sa'd verdict above.** In the *Imami* corpus this is a clause of the ***Wasiyyat al-Nabi li-Abi Dharr***, al-Tusi's *Amali* → **Bihar 74:77** — i.e. prophetic within his own school (late, chainless). The Sunni critics' verdict recorded earlier stands for the **Sunni transmission only**. Both facts should be stated.
- **Nahj Khutbah 206 continues past the prohibition to a *substitution* clause** («لو وصفتم أعمالهم…»). Speakers routinely stop early; the continuation is the constructive half.

## ⭐ Where his maxims actually come from — a structural finding

Nine of the *karam* maxims used across **Parts 14-16** map **entry-for-entry, in order**, onto a single compilation: entries **#4, #8, #11, #14, #26, #27, #28, #35, #36** of the al-Shi'a / Rayshahri ***al-Karam*** topical file.
