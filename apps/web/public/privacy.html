Privacy policy

OnePlate · version 1 · applies to the self-hosted service run by the household owner.

What OnePlate stores

Your email address and a password hash (or passkey public key) for sign-in; your household's entries: daily habits, fasts, mood, sleep, gratitude, journal lines, weigh-ins and children's growth measurements, meals, Quran progress and notes; an audit log of who changed what and when, with the IP address of the request.

Why

Only to run the app for your household. There is no advertising, no analytics service, no tracking pixel and no sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Who can see it

Members of your household according to the access level the owner gives them (owner, contributor, viewer); the person who operates the server, who can read the database; and nobody else. Children have no accounts; their data belongs to the household.

Health data

Weight, fasting, mood and sleep are health-related. OnePlate is an educational tool and not a medical device; nothing in it replaces advice from a doctor. Children are never given calorie targets or weight goals.

Retention and deletion

Entries stay until you delete them. Audit events are deleted after 365 days, expired sessions and reset links within a day. The household owner can export everything as JSON and delete the whole household at any time from the Account card; deletion is immediate and permanent.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; sessions use httpOnly cookies; connections are encrypted when the server is run behind TLS as shipped; every write is logged. Report a security problem to the operator before disclosing it.

Contact

The server operator is the household owner unless this page has been edited to say otherwise.
